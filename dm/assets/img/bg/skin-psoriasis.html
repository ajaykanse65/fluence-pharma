FAQ's

How do I know if I have Skin Psoriasis?

The symptoms will vary from person to person so here are some common symptoms of Psoriasis:

  • Itchy, scaly patches on the skin
  • Swollen Painful joints
  • Rashes
  • Dry, scaly and Cracked skin that can cause bleeding
  • Burning sensations or soreness around patches
  • Thick, pitted nails

Causes of Skin Psoriasis?

Psoriasis is an autoimmune disease, which means that a portion of the body's immune system overreacts and starts attacking healthy tissues.

  • Genetic
  • Obesity
  • Smoking
  • Stress

Patients with psoriasis may experience serious health problems such type 2 diabetes, heart disease, inflammatory bowel disease, psoriatic arthritis, anxiety, and depression.

Is Skin Psoriasis curable or not?

Psoriasis is a common, chronic, noncommunicable skin disease, with no clear cause or cure. On the other hand, a number of treatments can help control the symptoms and with just creams, about 75% of skin involvement gets better.

Myths and Facts

  • Myth - Psoriasis is caused by poor hygiene
    • Fact - Because psoriasis is a skin disease, many people mistakenly believe that poor hygiene is at the root of the red, scaly patches. Most people with this condition have a genetic tendency to develop psoriaisi. Other factors such a stress, injury, hormones and some medications can worsen psoriasis but do not cause it.

  • Myth - Psoriasis is Contagious
    • Fact - Because psoriasis patches are red and can crack and bleed, some people think that the skin is infected and can be contagious. This is not true. Psoriasis is an autoimmune disease, not an infection, and you cannnot “catch” psoriasis by toching or being in contact with someone with the condition.
