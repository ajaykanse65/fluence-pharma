FAQ's

How do I know if I have skin psoriasis?

The symptoms may vary from person to person. People may experience -

  • Itchy, scaly patches on the skin
  • Swollen Painful joints
  • Rashes
  • Dry, scaly & cracked skin that can cause bleeding
  • Burning sensations or soreness around patches
  • Thick, pitted nails

What is the cause of psoriasis?

Psoriasis is an autoimmune disease, which means that a portion of the body’s immune system overreacts & starts attacking healthy tissues. But for some people inherit genes that make them more likely to develop psoriasis.

Is skin psoriasis curable or not?

Psoriasis is a common, chronic, non-communicable skin disease with no clear cause or cure. A number of treatments are available which can help to aid the symptoms of psoriasis.

Myths & Facts

  • Myth - Psoriasis is caused by poor hygiene

    • Fact - Because psoriasis is a skin disease, many people mistakenly believe that poor hygiene is at the root of the red, scaly patches. Most people with this condition have a genetic tendency to develop psoriaisi. Other factors such a stress, injury, hormones & some medications can worsen psoriasis but do not cause it.


  • Myth - Psoriasis is Contagious

    • Fact - Because psoriasis patches are red & can crack & bleed, some people think that the skin is infected & can be contagious. This is not true. Psoriasis is an autoimmune disease, not an infection, & you cannnot “catch” psoriasis by toching or being in contact with someone with the condition.
