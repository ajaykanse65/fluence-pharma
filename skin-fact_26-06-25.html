About Skin

Problem associated with skin are resistant and persistent. Hence more and more drug intervention is required to address the same. Outcome of any treatment depends upon cellular response of the patient. Poor cellular response is responsible for increase resistant and persistence of problem.

We try improve the cellular response by providing nutritional supplements with clinically established protocol which deliver wellness, good health.

Problems

Pigmentation
Pigmentation is caused by an increase in melanin. A number of factors can trigger an increase in melanin production, but the main ones are sun exposure, hormonal influences, age and skin injuries or inflammation.
Skin acne
The skin has small pores that can become blocked by oil, dead skin cells, and dirt that get infected by bacteria P.acnes. When this occurs, one may develop a pimple or “zit.” also known as. it’s a most common problem seen.
Psoriasis
Psoriasis is defined as a persistent skin disease that causes the cell to build rapidly on the surface of the skin forming tick silvery scales, itchy dry and red patches, most commonly on the knees, elbows, trunk and scalp. Its a long-term (chronic) disease with no cure.
atopic dermatitis
Atopic dermatitis (eczema) is a condition that makes your skin red and itchy. its a chronic problem.
Vitiligo
Vitiligo occurs when pigment-producing cells die or stop functioning.Loss of skin colour can affect any part of the body, including the mouth, hair and eyes. It may be more noticeable in people with darker skin.Treatment may improve the appearance of the skin but doesn't cure the disease.
Signs of ageing
Few signs of ageing are Fine lines and wrinkles. wrinkles are the most evident and often most concern-causing signs of ageing for men and women, Dullness of skin, Uneven skin tone, Dry skin.
Teen acne problem
More than 85% of teenagers have much common , which is marked by clogged pores (whiteheads, blackheads), painful pimples, on the face, neck, shoulders, chest, back, and upper arms.

Pattern hair loss is a slow, gradual hair loss from specific areas. A small percentage of weak hair falls off for every cycle. In males it begins along the temporal corners and crown area.
