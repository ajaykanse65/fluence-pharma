PCOS, Stress

  • PCOS is a condition involving insulin resistance, increasing in oxidative stress and inflammation that leads to altered metabolism and relative hormonal imbalance resulting in hair loss.
    Cyclical nutrient therapy helps to build up the immune system and counters inflammation. It restores cellular function and supports overall recovery.
  • Stress and free radicals alters the metabolism and hormonal imbalance in pcos patients.
  • The conversion of testosterone to DHT causes the hair loss. Studies recognized that DHT releases TGFβ which is responsible for miniaturization. Now we know that the release of TGFβ is mediated by ROS. Latent TGFβ in the extracellular matrix is also activated by ROS. Experiments have revealed that using free radical scavenger to neutralize the ROS can block the release of TGFβ, thus countering the action of DHT.
  • Stress induced hair loss is mediated by substance P which is known to act through ROS. Antioxidants can counteract the effects of ROS and mitigate hair loss.
  • Stress leads to neurochemical, neurotransmitter and hormonal alterations through sympathetic nervous system, hypothalamic – pituatory – adrenal (HPA) axis and substance P, mediated by stress.
  • Elevation in allostatic load (chronic stress lead to the wear and tear of cells) causes sleep, deprivation, increase appetite, increase blood pressure, risk of cardiovascular disease and metabolic syndrome.
  • Stress escalates physiological demand for energy, oxygen utilization, cellular functions, requiring enzymes, cofactors, catalysts and micronutrients.
