Gut issue, Stress

  • Stress and depression affect the gut barrier permeability. The leaky gut allows inflammatory bacterial toxins to enter the circulation, promoting inflammation in various other cells and organs. Stress and depression relay distress signals to the gut through autonomic nerve endings, causing dysbiosis and a leaky gut. Raised cortisol level triggers insomnia.
  • The gut microbe is essential for the digestion and utilization of a variety of nutrients. Digestion and absorption both are interdependent. The lack of absorption of nutrient associated with poor absorption through the small intestinal mucosa.
  • Leaky gut is associated with an increase in chronic inflammation throughout the body, as well as reduced absorption of vitamins and minerals.
  • Irritable Bowel Syndrome or IBS is a chronic medical condition that affects over 7% of the general population. Symptoms like pain, bloating, diarrhea and constipation can severely affect life quality for patients and are often associated with the disruption of the digestive process.
  • Patients with IBS have been reported hair loss, hair thinning and brittle hair as part of the accompanying non-digestive symptoms.
