Insulin Resistance - Low iron/Low immunity

  • Insulin Resistance can develop from impaired glucose, amino acid and fat metabolism from ROS induced cellular dysfunction.
  • Diabetes reduces chemotaxis or movement of leucocytes, phagocytosis and generation of antibodies hampering immunity.
  • Oxidative stress is the main driver of insulin resistance. Pancreatic cells have very low free radical quenching enzymes making them vulnerable to apoptosis from ROS. Deficiencies of vitamin and minerals aggravate glucose intolerance.
  • Iron deficiency is the world’s most common nutritional deficiency and associated with developmental delay, increased risk of infection and insulin resistance.
  • Iron is well known for its benefits in hair growth. Iron deficiency is conventially suspected to cause increased hair shedding and telogen effluvium in female hair loss and diffused hair loss as well.
  • Iron deficiency affects metabolic functions in multiple cells, organ and systems.
  • Iron deficiency as well as high ferritin can both trigger cellular stress and autophagy. Iron from the follicles is reabsorbed in deficiency states. Low ferritin level leads to metabolic disorders and insulin resistance.
