Why do we rotate kits in Cyclical Therapy?

Revitalize, Rotate
Certain nutrients when supplied for a period lead to saturation in the cells and decline of response over time, which can be prevented and corrected by rotating the combinations in the kits.
Responsive Rotation
Individuals inherently respond better to one nutrient than another. Rotating the kits provides exposure to different nutrient combinations one after another.
Adaptive Kits
Individual variations in response to the same nutrient due to gene expression, covert deficiencies and variations in metabolic functions by birth. Different kits provide new proportions of nutrients which cover for these variations in subsequent cycles.
Targeted Rotation
Patients may have undetected, subclinical covert deficiencies which require correction and not all are included in each six day rotation. Hence rotating the kits with different combinations provides to take care of other possible deficiencies.
