Why do some adults have acne?

Although the hormonal fluctuations that cause acne are most common during the teen years, they can also affect adults. Women may experience hormonal swings during their menstrual cycle, pregnancy, and menopause that result in acne breakouts.

Acne can also be a side effect of certain medications, such as OC.

Does acne mean I’m not keeping my face clean enough?
Not necessarily. Scrubbing your face too hard or washing too frequently can aggravate your acne, and using alcohol-based astringents can dry out the skin. Acne is triggered by hormonal imbalance, and while gentle, regular cleansing with soap cleanser can sometimes help with mild breakouts; severe, recurrent acne requires more than just good hygiene.
Should I stop wearing makeup if I have acne?
If you suspect that your cosmetics may be contributing to your acne, try to spend a few weeks makeup-free and observe the results. Direct skin contact with greasy or oily substances and, in some cases, cosmetics that are applied directly to the skin may cause an acne breakout.
What other lifestyle changes would you recommend to keep acne at bay?
Get Active! Exercising promotes skin cell health by increasing blood flow. This increase in blood flow provides both oxygen and nutrients to cells and carries away waste products. In addition, exercise has been proven to alleviate stress, which can cause unpleasant effects on the complexion such as acne. Try yoga, which may help keep those stress levels down and your skin looking radiant.
Is there a correlation between food and acne?
Both inflammation and excess sebum can lead to acne. While these findings show that following a low-glycemic diet can lead to fewer breakouts, other studies have not found a substantial connection between a high-glycemic diet and acne.
Why is there recurrence and persistence in acne and how do we overcome it.
The erratic lifestyle, high stress levels, lack of exercise, obesity and high consumption of processed foods lead to disturbance in the hormonal changes, increased cortisol levels and disrupted cell milieu. These constant factors create an environment for persistent, recurrent acne.
Pigmentation
How do I avoid pigmentation?

If you want to protect your skin from pigmentation, you must avoid too much exposure to the sun. If your work requires you to go out in the sun, and avoiding it is not possible, use sunscreens and creams that help protect your skin from UVA and UVB rays. Besides that, you can also wear hats, sunglasses and clothes that protect your skin from the sun.

Some home remedies to get rid pigmentation.
Some of the most effective treatments for pigmentation can be prepared right in the comfort of your home.. Rubbing a slice of lemon on the affected area, for instance, is one effective DIY treatment for pigmentation. Similarly, natural products such as milk, Aloe vera, and honey also help in pigmentation treatment.
How else can i get rid of pigmentation?

Besides the above mentioned home remedies, there are various other solutions that can help get rid of pigmentation such as cosmetics, medical procedures, medications, and chemical peels. Cosmetics, for instance, are a temporary solution that can help you hide your dark spots or affected areas, immediately. Other options such as medication and medical treatments may require time, before you get enduring results.
