Transforming Health
through Cellular Restoration and Nutritional Support

Hair being a tissue with a high metabolic rate, even a modest imbalance affects the hair growth cycle converting mitosis to apoptosis.
It is also a non essential organ which means during triage hair will be prioritized last.

Nutrients are required for generation of energy, neutralization and elimination of toxins, repair of cellular organelles, repair of DNA, replacement of damaged cells and achieving rapid cell division for hair growth.
Nutrients are also responsible for secretion of hormones, neurotransmitters, chemical mediators, regulators of cell function, defense and immunity.

In condition of nutrient deficiencies, the body prioritizes short term survival over long term health. Non availability of even one single nutrient affects the continuity of biological actions.
ROS generated through cellular metabolism and externally induced by pollution, UV rays or scalp microbes can lead to cellular damage & aging. ROS influence hair follicles stem cells.

Diverse Hair Loss Phenotypes

Hair follicles being asynchronous individual mini organs react with variable response, creating combination and distribution of temporary or progressive hair loss, presenting as resolving telogen effluvium , continuing chronic telogen effluvium , diffuse hair loss AGA , scarring alopecia all phenotypes arise from cellular dysfunction.

Revitalizing Cellular Function

Conventional medicines aim at identifying disease & treating symptoms. Our approach is to restore cellular function. We are providing a comprehensive & scientific nutritional environment to thrive, repair & regenerate delivering optimized cellular response.We are focusing on strengthening hair follicles, reducing hair loss ensuring new hair growth & restoring the natural balance.
